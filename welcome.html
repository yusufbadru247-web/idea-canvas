Idea Canvas is a board with no edges. Put notes, links, photos, to-do lists, tables and mind maps side by side, draw lines between them, and find everything again from your phone.

Open the board

It opens in your browser. Boards are saved there, no account needed.

Columns for text, choices, dates, numbers and checkboxes, with colours and icons. The card on the board shows the whole table, not a cut-off preview.

AI on your cards

Select a few cards and ask for a summary, the next steps, the tasks hidden inside them, a table or a mind map. The answer lands on the board, linked to where it came from.

Made for the phone too
